Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

1. Who we are

The data controller is Davide Decastro, personal trainer, operating Decastro PT. Site: decastropt.com. Email: davide@decastrotraininglab.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Identity/contact: name, email, phone
  • Physical data: sex, age, weight, height
  • Health data: injuries, medications, sleep, stress, training goals — special category data collected only with your explicit consent
  • Usage data: pages viewed, device type, referrer, via Google Analytics 4 (anonymised IP, limited data usage) and Cloudflare analytics
  • Cookies: technical cookies and analytics cookies after consent

3. Legal basis

  • Performance of a contract / pre-contract: to reply to your request and plan your training.
  • Consent: for health data and for non-essential analytics cookies.
  • Legitimate interest: site security, fraud prevention and technical diagnostics.

4. How we use your data

  • To prepare your free assessment and personalised training plan.
  • To contact you and schedule appointments.
  • To send service-related communications.
  • To analyse aggregated, non-identifiable traffic and improve the site.

5. International transfers

We use service providers based in the EU and the US (Vercel, Resend, Calendly, Google). Where data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent adequacy decisions. US providers support GDPR-compatible processing and, where requested, EU data residency.

6. Third parties

Data is shared only with providers strictly necessary to run the service: hosting (Vercel), email delivery (Resend), scheduling (Calendly), analytics (Google Analytics 4), CDN and security (Cloudflare). Each provider is contractually bound to use data only on our behalf.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, port or object to the processing of your data. You may also withdraw consent at any time. Requests: davide@decastrotraininglab.com.

EEA/UK residents: you may lodge a complaint with your national Data Protection Authority (e.g. Garante Privacy in Italy, ICO in the UK).

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, and to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information.

Canada residents (PIPEDA): you have the right to access and challenge the accuracy of your personal information.

8. Cookies

The site uses:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — no consent required.
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4) — loaded only after you click "Accept" in the cookie banner. You can change or withdraw consent anytime below.

9. Data retention

Assessment form data is kept for up to 24 months, then deleted. Analytics data is retained according to Google and Cloudflare settings, anonymised where possible.

10. Security

Connections use HTTPS/TLS. Access to personal data is limited to Davide Decastro. No system is 100% secure; if we become aware of a data breach affecting you, we will notify you promptly.

11. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us to delete it.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always at this URL. Significant changes will be notified via the website.
