FAQ

How fast do I get my program?

Within 48-72 working hours you receive the program as a PDF, ready to take to the gym.

Do I need a full gym?

No. I adapt the program to what you have, whether a full gym or a basic home setup.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Programs work in the gym, at home, with or without equipment — and in hotel gyms when you travel.

Can I start with a single program?

Absolutely. Many start with one program, then move to the progressive path. The price you paid is deducted from the package.

I have back problems — can I train?

Tell me in the form or during the assessment: I adapt exercises and loads so you train safely without sacrificing results.

How much does it cost?

Assessment €50, custom program €80, monthly follow-up €30. The first consultation is always free.
