What's in your program

A custom technical program is not a generic list of exercises downloaded from the internet. It's a structured document built on your assessment data and your specific goal. Every parameter has a precise, measurable reason.

Exercise selection

Every exercise is chosen based on your level, available equipment and goal. For strength we use fundamental patterns (squat, deadlift, bench, rows); for hypertrophy, isolation and accessory schemes. Training at home? I swap dumbbells for bands or bodyweight without losing stimulus. No exercise is placed by chance.

Sets, reps & recovery

Volume (sets × reps) and intensity (% of 1RM or RPE) are calculated on your current level. A beginner starts at 3×8-12 RPE 6-7; an intermediate works 4×6 blocks at RPE 8; an advanced athlete uses undulating periodization. Recovery times run from 90 seconds for hypertrophy to 3-5 minutes for maximal strength. Every number is written in the program, not left to chance.

Weekly progression

The program covers 4-8 weeks with a defined linear or undulating progression. Week 1 sets the baseline; weeks 2-4 raise load 2-5% or cut recovery; weeks 5-6 introduce time-under-tension variation. Progressive overload is programmed, not improvised — you see exactly what to do each week.

Deload weeks

Every 4-6 weeks the program includes a deload: volume cut 40-50%, intensity held at 70% of 1RM. It's there to recover from accumulated fatigue, reduce injury risk and allow supercompensation. It's part of the program, already written in.

Accessory & mobility work

After the main lifts the program includes 2-3 accessories: rotator cuff, core stabilizers, posterior chain, hip and thoracic mobility. Not fillers — they correct imbalances found in the assessment and prevent injury. If you sit 8 hours a day, the program includes mobility protocols for your specific stiffness pattern.

Home, gym or hybrid

The program adapts to where you train, not the other way around. A full gym, hotel gym, two dumbbells or bodyweight only — the programming holds. If you travel, your program travels with you.

FAQ

How long does a program last?

A custom technical program typically covers 4-8 weeks, with weekly progressions defined. It's updated after the follow-up.

Do I need the assessment first?

Strongly recommended. The assessment provides the base data (BMI, BFR, BMR, TDEE) the protocol is built on. Without the numbers, the margin of error is larger.

Do you work with beginners?

Yes, all levels. For beginners I build programs that teach correct movement before load.

Experience with injuries?

Yes. I've worked with clients in post-injury reathletization (shoulder, knee, lower back). The program adapts to your limits.

How fast do I get my program?

After receiving your answers, the program is delivered as a PDF within 48-72 working hours, ready to take to the gym.
